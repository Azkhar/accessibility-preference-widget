Try page-level preferences

Open the floating preference button and experiment with typography, contrast, focus, cursor, reading, and motion options.

Preferences are stored locally in this browser. This demo makes no application-level service requests.

Sample content

Typography

Change text size, line height, spacing, alignment, and font family.

Visual preferences

Try contrast modes, monochrome presentation, and focus indicators.

Reading support

Use the reading guide, larger cursor, and link or heading highlights.

Important limitation

This widget does not by itself make a website WCAG-conformant. Site-wide design, content, code, and assistive-technology testing are still required.

Demo content is included locally in this repository.
